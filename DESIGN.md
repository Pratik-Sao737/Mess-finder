# Fidelity Dark Serif Design System

## Brand & Style
The design system embraces a **Modern Editorial** style, combining high-end print typography with a robust, reliable digital interface. It evokes a sense of intellectual authority, precision, and sophistication.

## Colors
- **Color Mode**: Dark
- **Primary**: `#3366cc` (Royal Blue)
- **Secondary**: `#54595d` (Slate Gray)
- **Tertiary**: `#fee77e` (Pale Yellow)
- **Neutral**: `#202122` (Dark Slate)

## Typography
- **Headlines**: Noto Serif
- **Body**: Inter
- **Labels**: Public Sans

## Layout & Spacing
Built on a fluid grid model with generous margins (`2rem`) and consistent component spacing increments.

## Elevation & Depth
Utilizes tonal surface layering and low-contrast outlines tailored specifically for dark mode environments.

## Shapes
Soft rounded corners (`0.25rem` base) maintaining a crisp, professional aesthetic.

## Components
Standardized components including buttons, chips, inputs, and structured cards designed for optimal readability and interaction in dark environments.
